Help & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions regarding setup, channels, and compatibility.

How do JUST CLICK TV subscriptions work?
Once you purchase a subscription (1 Year or 2 Year), our system immediately generates your M3U playlist link, Xtream API credentials, or MAC address activation. If you ordered a TV Box, it arrives pre-configured and ready to plug and play.
Which countries and channels are available?
We cover live TV channels and VOD content across Arabic, English, Italian, Indian, Turkish, Persian, German, African, French, Spanish, and many more. Our packages include premium sports networks, news, documentary, movies, and children channels.
Do subscriptions work directly on Smart TVs without a box?
Yes! You can use our Smart TV Subscription package directly on Samsung, LG, Sony, or Android TVs using applications like Smart IPTV, IBO Player, TiviMate, or IPTV Smarters Pro.
How do I renew my current subscription?
You can select any subscription package from our site and enter your existing username or MAC address during checkout, or reach out to our WhatsApp support team for instant 1-click renewal.
How do I contact customer support if I need setup help?
We provide 24/7 support via our Contact Form, Email (support@justclicktv.com), and instant floating WhatsApp chat on our website.
